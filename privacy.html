Privacy Policy

Apex Data Engineering specializes in forensic B2B data processing. We collect and process business-related information from publicly available sources to facilitate commercial growth for our clients.

Under CCPA, we respect the privacy rights of business owners. Our data collection is limited to professional metadata and publicly disclosed contact information. We do not process sensitive personal data or non-business related identifiers.

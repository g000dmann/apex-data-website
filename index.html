Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our data.

Are your lists CCPA & GDPR compliant?
Yes. We strictly collect B2B data from public business registries, professional networks, and corporate websites. We do not aggregate private consumer data, ensuring full compliance for cold outbound campaigns.
How fresh is the data?
Our lists are generated entirely on-demand. When you request a batch, our engine scans the live web in real-time, ensuring zero decay and maximum deliverability.
Do you sell the same list to my competitors?
No. Every batch we engineer is exclusively generated for the client who requested it based on their specific geo-targets and niche requirements. You won't be competing with 50 other agencies emailing the same list.
What happens if an email bounces?
Every address is SMTP-verified before delivery, so hard bounces are rare. If one still bounces, send us the address and we replace it 1:1 free of charge — that is our bounce guarantee. No ticket ping-pong, no extra invoice.
What exactly is the data quality score?
Each lead is graded 0–100 from independently measured signals: email verification, Google rating and review count, Google Business completeness, and the live website defect audit (HTTPS, meta description, H1, alt text). You see the score before you buy.

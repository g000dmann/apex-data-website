Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our data.

Are your lists CCPA & GDPR compliant?
Yes. We strictly collect B2B data from public business registries, professional networks, and corporate websites. We do not aggregate private consumer data, ensuring full compliance for cold outbound campaigns.
How fresh is the data?
Every batch is drawn from our own lead database and re-screened against our suppression and bounce lists right before delivery. Each record carries its email-verification status, so you see exactly what was checked — no recycled third-party list.
Do you sell the same list to my competitors?
No. Every batch we engineer is exclusively generated for the client who requested it based on their specific geo-targets and niche requirements. You won't be competing with 50 other agencies emailing the same list.
What happens if an email bounces?
Every address is syntax- and domain-checked (DNS/MX) and screened against our suppression and bounce lists before delivery, and every record ships with its verification status. If one still hard-bounces, send us the address and we replace it 1:1 free of charge — that is our bounce guarantee. No ticket ping-pong, no extra invoice.
What exactly do I get on each record?
Company name, website, city and state, the contact email we hold plus its verification status, the Google rating and review count, the opportunity and urgency note, and a LinkedIn profile where we found one. The 0–100 website health score belongs to our website audit report, not to the lead file.
